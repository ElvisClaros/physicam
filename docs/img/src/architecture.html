How Physicam works

A phone video plus a question becomes a measurement with error bars. Every number comes from a tool, never from the model's imagination.
MUSE VOICE

Video + question

Upload a clip (60–240 fps) and ask in plain language, typed or spoken:

“What's the coefficient of restitution?”

MUSE SPARK · VIDEO

Watch & plan

Spark watches the clip with audio. It identifies the phenomenon, the objects to track (as SAM prompts) and reference objects of known size, and hears spoken facts (“the string is 80 cm”).

MUSE SPARK · AGENT SAM 3.1

Measure like a physicist
